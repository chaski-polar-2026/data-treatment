# Species data, first review batch

This branch contains the first-priority batch only: anchoveta, jumbo flying squid, Peruvian scallop, Peruvian octopus, Peruvian hake, and cachema. The batch is intentionally incomplete where a checked source was not available; empty values are not estimates.

## Method

Scientific names were checked against WoRMS or the Peru-focused PeRMS list on 2026-11-14. Species notes are short English summaries. Each populated species field is linked to one or more source records in especies_fuentes.csv; source records and consultation dates are in fuentes.csv.

## Sources checked

WoRMS taxon records; the IMARPE biodiversity catalog and research repository; the IMARPE fisheries article on Dosidicus gigas; IMARPE material on anchoveta distribution during El Niño; and IMARPE scallop habitat and population-assessment material.

## Limits and pending work

Temperature ranges and optima, seasonality, precise coast ranges for most taxa, species-level landings endpoints and coverage, and direct AphiaID verification for Cynoscion analis and Merluccius peruanus remain pending. Do not infer fishing probabilities or current occurrence from habitat/depth ranges. The submitted Merluccius gayi peruanus was corrected to the WoRMS accepted name Merluccius peruanus; its AphiaID must be confirmed before release. The first-priority records require biological review before work proceeds to the next species group.

No bulk landing data were downloaded. The landing-data table records discovery leads only, not confirmed datasets.
